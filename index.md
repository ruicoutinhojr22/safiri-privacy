# Privacy Policy for Safiri Driver

**Last updated:** September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Safiri Driver ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile app Safiri Driver.

## Information We Collect
- **Personal Information:** Name, email, phone number, profile photo.
- **Location Data:** Precise GPS location, including background location, to enable trip tracking and connect drivers with riders.
- **Device Information:** Device model, OS, unique identifiers, IP address.
- **Usage Data:** Trip history and in-app activity.

## How We Use Your Information
- To operate the Safiri Driver service
- To connect drivers with riders
- To track trips and calculate fares
- To ensure driver and passenger safety
- To comply with legal obligations

## Background Location
Safiri Driver collects location in the background to enable continuous trip tracking. You can disable this in device settings, but functionality will be limited.

## Sharing
We do not sell your data. We share with riders (as needed for trips), service providers (hosting, maps), and law enforcement when required by law.

## Data Retention
We retain data while your account is active or as required by law.

## Your Rights
Contact us at support@mymobisolutions.com to access, correct, or delete your data.

## Children's Privacy
Not intended for users under 18.

## Security
We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information.
